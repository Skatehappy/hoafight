## California (CA)
- **Primary statute:** Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act — Cal. Civ. Code §§ 4000-6150
- **Sources (5):**
  - [primary_hoa_statute / records_access] https://legalclarity.org/california-hoa-law-davis-stirling-and-homeowner-rights/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Davis-Stirling Act is Civ. Code §§ 4000-6150 (enacted 1985); §§ 5200-5240 give record-inspection rights; § 5210 (updated effective Jan 1, 2026) sets 10-business-day (current FY) and 30-calendar-day (prior two FYs) production deadlines.
  - [fine_limits] https://www.fmglaw.com/hoa/important-changes-for-hoas-hoa-fines-are-now-capped-and-other-new-requirements/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Civ. Code § 5850(c) caps fines at $100 per violation; higher fine only for health/safety risk with written board finding at open meeting; 10+ days' hearing notice and written decision within 15 days required.
  - [last_major_reform / fine_limits] https://www.hbblaw.com/hoa-fines-capped-at-100-immediate-impact-of-californias-ab-130/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - AB 130 signed June 30, 2025, effective immediately; amended Civ. Code § 5850 to cap fines at lesser of schedule amount or $100 per violation, bans late fees/interest, health-safety exception under § 5850(d); pre-June-30-2025 fines unaffected.
  - [records_access (penalty) / dispute_resolution] https://findhoalaw.com/civil-code-section-5235-enforcement-of-records-inspection-rights/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Civ. Code § 5235: court may assess civil penalty up to $500 per denied written request plus mandatory reasonable costs and attorney's fees; action may be brought in small claims court without prior ADR request.
  - [meeting_requirements] https://findhoalaw.com/civil-code-section-4920-notice-of-board-meetings/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Civ. Code § 4920: 4+ days' notice for regular board meetings; 2+ days for nonemergency executive-session-only meetings; no notice for emergency meetings; notice must contain agenda; executive session topics enumerated in § 4935.

## Texas (TX)
- **Primary statute:** Texas Residential Property Owners Protection Act (TRPOPA) — Tex. Prop. Code Title 11, Chapter 209 (§§ 209.001 et seq.)
- **Sources (6):**
  - [primary_hoa_statute / records_access] https://codes.findlaw.com/tx/property-code/prop-sect-209-005/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - § 209.005: association must send inspection-date notice or produce copies on or before the 10th business day after receiving request; if unable, alternate date no later than 15th business day; board must adopt a recorded records production and copying policy before charging for copies.
  - [fine_limits] https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/property-code/title-11/chapter-209/section-209-006/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - § 209.006: HOA must give written notice before enforcement action describing violation, intended action, and owner's right to a hearing; notice/hearing rights don't re-apply to same violation within preceding six months (§ 209.006(d)).
  - [fine_limits (no cap)] https://fixmyhoaviolation.com/blog/texas-hoa-fine-limits-2026 (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Texas imposes no statewide dollar cap on HOA fines; fines must be authorized by governing documents and reasonable; hearing rights under §§ 209.006-209.007 (30-day request window, hearing within 30 days, 10-day advance hearing notice).
  - [meeting_requirements] https://codes.findlaw.com/tx/property-code/prop-sect-209-0051/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - § 209.0051: regular/special board meetings open to owners; 144-hour notice for regular and 72-hour notice for special meetings; votes and final decisions must be made in open session.
  - [regulatory_body / complaint_process] https://www.hopb.co/texas-hoa-questions-and-answers (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Texas has no state agency regulating HOAs; AG handles only limited matters; disputes resolved via justice/small claims court, district court, or private mediation.
  - [last_major_reform] https://fieldslawtx.com/2025-texas-legislative-update-key-changes-impacting-hoas/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - 2025 89th Legislature: SB 711 reforms to Chapters 82/202/209 (website posting of dedicatory instruments for 60+ unit/managed associations, TREC-filing updates), eff. Sept. 1, 2025; SB 2586 fee-schedule posting; SB 2629 electronic voting. 2023: HB 614 added § 209.0060; HB 886 assessment-lien delinquency notices.

## Florida (FL)
- **Primary statute:** Florida Homeowners' Association Act — Fla. Stat. Chapter 720
- **Sources (7):**
  - [primary_hoa_statute / records_access] https://legalclarity.org/florida-statute-720-303-hoa-requirements/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - § 720.303 requires official records be made available within 10 business days of written request, within 45 miles or the county; 7-year retention; 100+ parcel associations must post records to a website.
  - [records_access (penalty)] https://www.pmlawfla.com/how-to-request-and-inspect-hoa-records-under-florida-law/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Under § 720.303(5), failure to produce within 10 business days creates a rebuttable presumption of willful noncompliance; $50/day statutory damages up to $500 accrue plus attorney's fees; § 718.111(12)(c) parallel condo remedy.
  - [fine_limits] https://effortlesshoa.com/blog/hoa-laws-florida (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Fines capped at $100 per violation per day, not to exceed $1,000 aggregate for a single continuous violation; fines over $100 require a hearing before a committee of at least three members who are not board members or relatives; 14-day notice required.
  - [fine_limits (caps + hearing details)] https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/720.305 (retrieved 2026-11-01; replaces competitor source fightmyhoa.com)
    - §720.305(2): $100/violation and $1,000 aggregate caps both apply "unless otherwise provided in the governing documents"; 14 days' written notice of the right to a hearing; hearing within 90 days before a 3+ member independent committee that may only confirm or reject; written findings within 7 days; payment due no sooner than 30 days after that notice.
  - [fine_limits (2024 amendments)] https://www.flsenate.gov/session/Bill/2024/1203 (retrieved 2026-11-01)
    - CS/HB 1203, effective July 1, 2024 (90-day hearing, 7-day findings, 30-day payment rules).
  - [regulatory_body / complaint_process] https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/condos-timeshares-mobile-homes/homeowners-associations/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - The DBPR Division does NOT have statutory authority to investigate complaints against HOAs under Chapter 720; its Ch. 720 authority is limited to arbitrating election and recall disputes.
  - [dispute_resolution] https://www.flsenate.gov/laws/statutes/2024/720.311 (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - § 720.311 mandates pre-suit mediation for covenant/parcel-use, document-amendment, meeting, and records disputes; excludes assessment/fine collection; election and recall disputes are instead arbitrated by the DBPR division. Responding party has 20 days to respond.
  - [last_major_reform / unique_protections] https://www.varnumlaw.com/insights/changes-florida-hoas-house-bill-1203/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - HB 1203 (effective July 1, 2024) overhauled Ch. 720: director education, records/website transparency for 100+ parcel associations, fine changes, debit-card prohibition, audited financials for 1,000+ parcels, architectural-denial written-notice requirement, vegetable-garden and interior-of-home protections.

## New York (NY)
- **Primary statute:** No comprehensive single-family HOA statute. Most NY HOAs are incorporated as not-for-profit corporations governed by the Not-for-Profit Corporation Law (NPCL) plus their recorded declaration/CC&Rs; the Real Property Law and General Business Law add property-rights and disclosure duties. — N.Y. Not-for-Profit Corporation Law (NPCL), Consolidated Laws of NY, Chapter 35
- **Sources (4):**
  - [primary_hoa_statute / no comprehensive statute] https://ag.ny.gov/resources/individuals/tenants-homeowners/homeowners-associations (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - NY AG page confirms HOAs organized as non-profits are governed by the Not-for-Profit Corporation Law; AG requires sponsor to file/honor an offering plan; AG jurisdiction limited to ownership/maintenance of common property.
  - [records_access / NPCL §621] https://law.justia.com/codes/new-york/npc/article-6/621/ (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - NPCL §621 (2024): books/records and right of inspection; 5 days' written demand, member of record 6+ months; §621(e-1) HOA financial-records review right.
  - [condo_statute / RPL Art. 9-B] https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/A9-B (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - Article 9-B Condominium Act, RPL §339-d et seq.; declaration filing under §339-n subjects a condo to the Act (§339-f).
  - [dispute_resolution / small claims] https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/hoa-problems.pdf (retrieved 2026-07-22)
    - AG 'HOA Problems' guidance outlining the limited role of the state and directing owners to courts for governance disputes; NY applies business-judgment rule; $10,000 small-claims access.
