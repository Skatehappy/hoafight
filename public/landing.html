FAQ

Common questions.

What if the rule is in the CC&Rs?
The letter focuses on whether the rule was applied fairly, whether proper procedure was followed, and whether the fine amount is proportionate — even valid rules can be appealed on procedural grounds.
Do I have the right to a hearing?
In most states, yes. Your HOA bylaws almost certainly require a hearing before fines become final. The letter demands this right explicitly.
What if the HOA ignores me?
The letter references your state's HOA ombudsman or regulatory agency — filing a complaint there is free and often prompts a response faster than legal action.
Does this work for condo associations too?
Yes. The AI handles both HOA and condo association disputes and cites the appropriate state statutes for each.
